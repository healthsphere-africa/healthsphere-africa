Typhoid Fever

Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi. It is usually spread when someone consumes food or water contaminated with the bacteria.

Typhoid can become serious, but early medical evaluation and appropriate treatment can greatly reduce the risk of complications.

⏱️ Typhoid Fever in One Minute

Typhoid fever is an infection that can enter the bloodstream after Salmonella Typhi is swallowed through contaminated food or water. Common symptoms include prolonged fever, weakness, headache, abdominal discomfort, loss of appetite and constipation or diarrhoea.

Diagnosis requires appropriate laboratory testing. Blood culture is an important diagnostic test, while treatment requires antibiotics selected by a healthcare professional.

🦠 What Is Typhoid Fever?

Typhoid fever is a systemic bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi.

The bacteria are adapted to humans and can spread when infected people or carriers contaminate food or water with the bacteria.

After entering the body, the bacteria can cross the intestinal barrier and spread through the bloodstream.

🔬 The Bacteria Behind Typhoid

The organism responsible for typhoid fever is Salmonella enterica serovar Typhi, commonly called Salmonella Typhi.

It is different from many forms of Salmonella that mainly cause gastrointestinal illness.

Typhoid is a systemic infection, meaning it can affect multiple parts of the body after the bacteria spread beyond the intestine.

🔄 How Typhoid Develops in the Body

1️⃣ Ingestion The bacteria enter the body through contaminated food or water.
2️⃣ Intestinal Entry The bacteria pass through the intestinal tract.
3️⃣ Spread The bacteria can spread into the bloodstream.
4️⃣ Illness The body's response contributes to fever and other symptoms.

🚰 How Typhoid Spreads

Typhoid is mainly transmitted through the faecal-oral route. This means bacteria from the faeces of an infected person or carrier can contaminate food or water and subsequently be swallowed by another person.

  • Unsafe drinking water
  • Food prepared with contaminated water
  • Poor hand hygiene after using the toilet
  • Unsafe food handling
  • Inadequate sanitation

🤒 Signs & Symptoms

Symptoms can vary from person to person. Common features include:

  • Fever, often lasting several days or longer
  • Weakness and fatigue
  • Headache
  • Abdominal pain or discomfort
  • Loss of appetite
  • Nausea
  • Constipation or diarrhoea
  • Sometimes cough
  • Sometimes a rash with small rose-coloured spots

🧠 Why Do These Symptoms Occur?

🌡️ Fever The body's immune response to infection contributes to an elevated temperature.
😴 Weakness Systemic infection and reduced appetite can leave the body fatigued.
🤕 Headache Headache can occur as part of the body's response to the infection.
🫃 Abdominal Symptoms The infection affects the intestinal tract and can cause abdominal discomfort and changes in bowel habits.

🧪 Diagnosis & Laboratory Tests

Typhoid symptoms can resemble several other illnesses that cause fever. Laboratory testing is therefore important.

🩸 Blood Culture Blood culture is a key laboratory method for identifying Salmonella Typhi.
🧪 Culture & Sensitivity Testing the organism can help healthcare professionals understand which antibiotics are likely to work.
⚠️ Widal Test Widal testing can produce false-positive results and should not be relied upon by itself to confirm acute typhoid fever.
Important: A person with fever should not assume that the illness is typhoid without appropriate medical assessment. Malaria and other infections can cause similar symptoms.

💊 Treatment & Management

Typhoid fever can be treated with antibiotics. However, antibiotic resistance is an increasing problem, so treatment should be guided by a qualified healthcare professional and, where appropriate, laboratory results.

  • Seek medical assessment when typhoid is suspected.
  • Use antibiotics only when prescribed.
  • Take prescribed treatment exactly as directed.
  • Complete the prescribed course unless a healthcare professional advises otherwise.
  • Continue good hand hygiene to reduce transmission.
HealthSphere Safety Note: Do not use this article to choose an antibiotic or dose for yourself. The appropriate treatment depends on the clinical situation and local antibiotic resistance patterns.

🛡️ Prevention

🚰 Safe Water Drink water from a safe source. Where necessary, use appropriately treated or boiled water.
🧼 Handwashing Wash hands thoroughly with soap, especially after using the toilet and before preparing or eating food.
🍲 Food Safety Eat properly cooked food and avoid food or drinks that may be contaminated.
💉 Vaccination Typhoid vaccines can help prevent typhoid fever and are recommended for appropriate groups according to local immunization guidance.

⚠️ Danger Signs

Typhoid can become severe. Seek urgent medical attention if someone with suspected or confirmed typhoid becomes seriously unwell, particularly with severe abdominal symptoms, confusion, persistent vomiting, bleeding, severe weakness, or other concerning changes.

👶 Typhoid in Children

Children can develop typhoid fever, particularly where safe water, sanitation and hygiene are inadequate.

A child with persistent fever or significant illness should be assessed by a healthcare professional rather than being assumed to have typhoid.

🤰 Typhoid During Pregnancy

Pregnancy requires particular attention when a significant infection is suspected. A pregnant person with persistent fever or symptoms suggestive of typhoid should seek medical assessment promptly.

Healthcare professionals can select investigations and treatment that take pregnancy into account.

💡 Did You Know?

1. Typhoid is caused by Salmonella Typhi, which is adapted to humans.
2. A person can continue to carry and shed the bacteria even after symptoms improve.
3. Antibiotic resistance is making some typhoid infections more difficult to treat.
4. Vaccination works best as part of a broader strategy that includes safe water, sanitation and food hygiene.

❌ Myths & Facts

MYTH: Every prolonged fever is typhoid.

FACT: Many illnesses can cause fever. Proper assessment and testing are important.
MYTH: A positive Widal test alone proves typhoid.

FACT: Widal testing can give misleading results and should not be used alone to confirm acute typhoid.
MYTH: Once symptoms disappear, the bacteria cannot spread.

FACT: Some people may continue carrying and shedding Salmonella Typhi.
MYTH: Typhoid vaccination replaces food and water hygiene.

FACT: Vaccination should be combined with safe water, sanitation and good food hygiene.

🫀 Typhoid & The Human Body

Typhoid begins through the gastrointestinal tract but can become a systemic infection after the bacteria enter the bloodstream.

🫃 Digestive System The bacteria enter through the intestinal tract.
🩸 Bloodstream The bacteria can spread through the blood.
🛡️ Immune System The body's immune response contributes to the illness and symptoms.
🧠 Nervous System Severe illness can sometimes affect mental status and brain function.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can typhoid be treated?

Yes. Typhoid fever can be treated with antibiotics prescribed by a healthcare professional.

Can malaria and typhoid have similar symptoms?

Yes. Fever and other symptoms can occur in several infections, which is why appropriate assessment and testing are important.

Can someone spread typhoid after feeling better?

Yes. Some people can continue to carry and shed Salmonella Typhi after their symptoms improve.

Can typhoid be prevented?

Yes. Safe water, sanitation, hand hygiene, food safety and appropriate vaccination all help reduce the risk.
